Hey, I'm Andrew

At age five, I wanted to be a robot when I grew up. Today, I build them.

Few things are as rewarding as tackling challenges I once thought unattainable. That drive has led me to establish a broad technical foundation across mechatronics, ranging from mechanical and electrical design for powered drive systems and mine-clearance equipment, to industry experience in embedded software and autonomous vehicle motion planning.

But technical capability is only half the equation. To balance the engineering side, I’ve spent significant time serving in people-first leadership roles focused on organization and team management.

I’m always eager to expand my expertise and see what ambitions become reachable next. Thanks for stopping by!
