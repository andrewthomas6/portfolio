Overview

Have you ever heard a profound bit of advice, or a line from a friend that had you smiling long after it was said? Many people record quotes, though recording them quickly and sharing them easily is a challenge. Quotable solves these issues.

Quotable began as an HTML/CSS and TypeScript site and was rebuilt as an iOS and Android app in the winter of 2026 to increase accessibility and traffic. The current app uses a React Native frontend and PHP backend. The beauty of an ongoing project like this is that once myself and my co-developers had laid the foundation, each new feature ticket has become a chance to dig further into the intricacies of UI/UX design. Having a clean software architecture has also been instrumental, as we've realized through a few large refactors. Our API and backend have involved into a systematic query and response system to cleanly access our SQL-based database. Hosting a live platform (and debugging it when it goes down) has given me hands on experience in networking and server management. While initially frustrating, failures certainly result in a better grasp of diagnostic tools and the finer details of integration. From deep dives into DNS records to managing an ever growing list of tasks and team members, Quotable has proven the benefits of tackling an ambitious idea and constantly pushing it forward.
